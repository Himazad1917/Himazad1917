Engineering approach

Evidence first. Claims kept honest.

Device-specific values are linked to manufacturer information where possible. Examples are illustrative, and conceptual diagrams are not presented as verified schematics.

For actual installations, the exact model manual, approved site procedures and qualified personnel take precedence.
